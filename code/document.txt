Company Remote Work Policy

Employees are allowed to work remotely up to three days per week.

Employees must submit leave requests at least two working days before
the requested leave date.

The standard working hours are from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

Employees working remotely must remain available during standard
working hours and attend all required meetings.

Remote work is subject to manager approval.

Employees must use the company's approved VPN when accessing internal
company systems remotely.
